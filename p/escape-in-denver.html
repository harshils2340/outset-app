What escape rooms is actually like

One locked room, one hour, and a puzzle trail that turns a group of friends into a team.

  1. Arrive 15 minutes early. Your game master briefs you on rules and how to ask for hints.
  2. Step into the room and the clock starts. Search everything: drawers, paintings, under the rug.
  3. Solve puzzles that unlock the next. Hints come through a screen or a walkie if you're stuck.
  4. Escape, or don't. Either way there's a group photo at the end and a debrief of what you missed.

Bring: Nothing special, Glasses if you need them for reading, A group of 2 to 8 is the sweet spot. Good for: Friends, dates, families with teens, and coworkers who need a shared enemy.

How much does it cost?
