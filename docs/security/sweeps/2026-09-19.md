**4. Hacked-page screen.** Copied `isCompromisedText`/`isCompromisedPhrase`
and their helpers (`SPAM_LINE`, `HACKED_SCRIPT_SPAM`, `isKeywordStuffed`,
`hasRepeatedSuspectWord`) verbatim from `backend/src/sync/contacts.ts`
(unchanged since the baseline) into a standalone script under `/tmp`, and
ran it over every string field, recursively, in all 59,126 operators in
`public/catalog.json` and all 59,125 files in `public/o/*.json`.

Flagged tonight: **12** in `catalog.json`, **20** in `public/o/*.json` —
down sharply from last night's 13 and 106. Diffing by id and by which rule
tripped:

- **13 of the 20** are genuine spam-banner cover photos, same pattern as
  every prior sweep: the crawl picked up a `cover`/`photos[0]` URL from an
  image host serving an obvious gambling-spam banner (`daftar-situs-togel`,
  `bandar-togel-online`, `TERJAMIN-MAXWIN`, `olympus-slot88`, and similar),
  on otherwise-real operators (a golf club, a distillery, a museum, a dance
  studio). These are the same shape of hit as the 106 named in the last two
  reports, just far fewer of them now.
- **7** are `isKeywordStuffed` hits on repeated text that reads as a
  scraping artifact or a real rate card, not gambling/hacked-page spam:
  `o-easttnscouts-org` (the same already-known Scout-camp false positive),
  `o-islandchillyachtcharters-com` (a repeated policy-list line),
  `o-locationdesquatrelacs-com` (a repeated boat rate card, the exact shape
  the function's own comment says it's tuned to allow — worth another look
  at the 0.35-of-field-length bar), `o-nortonslc-com` (leftover Wix
  placeholder copy, "really really really"), `o-rfcity-org` (a repeated
  nav breadcrumb, "Rental Housing" x5), and `o-scene75-com` (three service
  descriptions where a single word — "LASER TAG", "MINI BOWLING", "LASER
  MAZE" — repeats a few dozen times each, almost certainly a crawl/render
  bug duplicating one word rather than injected spam).

No spam phrase got past the existing patterns on any of the 13 genuine
hits, so no new phrase was needed tonight.

The sharp drop (106 to 20 in `public/o/`) lines up with this window's
crawl and publish-gate work (`45810cb24` "a listing reaches guests once we

listings. This sandbox can't confirm the mechanism (no production database
access), so treat it as observed, not explained; worth confirming on the
next sweep whether the count keeps falling or holds at 20 as a new steady
state, and note in Needs Harshil below.
