Questions

What operators ask first.

Does it actually book, or just take a message?

It books. Otto reads your live calendar, offers times that are really open, and confirms the reservation on the call. Anything outside what you've published, it takes a name and number instead of guessing.

Will it make something up?

No. Otto answers only from your published prices, hours, age rules and policies. If it doesn't know, it says so and takes a message. It also declines weather, directions and anything not about your business.

What booking systems does it work with?

The major ones operators already use, FareHarbor, Peek, Xola, Checkfront, Rezdy, Square, Acuity, and more. Not connected yet? Otto still answers every call from your published facts and takes the booking details for you.

How long does setup take?

About a day. You forward your calls or use a new number, we load your business, you test it. We do it with you.

What does it cost?

A simple monthly price per location, no long contract, cancel anytime. It pays for itself the first time it catches a booking you'd have missed. Pricing is set with our first operators.
