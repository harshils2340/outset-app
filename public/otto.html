Questions

What operators ask us first.

Does it really book, or just take a message?

It books. Otto reads your live booking calendar, offers the times that are actually open, and confirms the reservation on the call. When something is outside what you have published, it takes a name and number for you instead of guessing.

What booking systems does it work with?

Otto reads the major booking calendars operators already use, and we are adding more. If yours is not connected yet, Otto still answers every call from your published prices, hours and policies, and takes the booking details for you to confirm. Tell us your system and we will check.

Will it make something up?

No. Otto answers only from the facts you have published: your prices, hours, age rules and policies. If it does not know, it says so and takes a message. It also declines weather, directions and anything not about your business, and offers to pass the caller to a person.

How long does setup take?

About a day. You forward your calls or use a new number, we load your business, and you test it. We do it with you, by hand.

What does it cost?

A simple monthly price per location, no long contract. It pays for itself the first time it catches a booking you would have missed. We finalise pricing with our first operators.
