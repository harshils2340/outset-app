Questions

What operators ask first

Still have one? Talk to us

Does it replace my staff?

No. Your staff keep answering the phone the way they do now. Outset only picks up the calls they can't get to, when they're busy with customers or you're closed, so those callers get help instead of a voicemail. Anything that needs a person comes to you with a summary.

Who answers the phone?

An AI assistant we call Otto, answering in your business's name. It's the voice you hear in the sample call, and you can give yours any name you like.

Does it book, or just take a message?

It helps the caller book. It finds a time that works and sends them straight to your booking page. Anything outside what you've published goes to your team as a message instead of a guess.

Will it make something up?

No. It answers only from your published prices, hours, age rules and policies. If it doesn't know, it says so and takes a message. It also declines weather, directions and anything not about your business.

What booking systems does it work with?

It works alongside whatever you use today, including FareHarbor, Peek, Xola, Resova, Bookeo, Checkfront, Rezdy, ROLLER and Square. See the full list

How long does setup take?

About a day. Your missed calls forward to Outset, or you use a new number, we load your business, and you test it yourself. We do it with you.

What does it cost?

Setup is free. After that it's a simple monthly price per location, with no long contract and you can cancel anytime. It pays for itself the first time it catches a booking you'd have missed. See plans
