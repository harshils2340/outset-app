How it works

  1. 1. Otto gets your numberForward your calls, or give out a new line. Set-up takes a day, and nothing changes about how you already work.
  2. 2. Otto learns your businessYour hours, prices, age rules, what you offer and your real calendar. It answers from your own facts, and never makes anything up.
  3. 3. Otto answers and booksIt picks up on the first ring, any hour, answers what the caller asks, checks what is actually free and books them in. You get the booking and a summary of the call.

Why Otto is different

Most AI receptionists only take a message. Otto checks your real availability and books the slot. It is wired into the booking calendar you already use, so it knows what is free today and can take the reservation on the call, not just promise someone will call back.

What Otto handles

  • Do you have anything this Saturday?
  • How much for four people?
  • What time do you open?
  • Can my 8-year-old do it?
  • Do you take walk-ins?
  • Booking a time and party size
  • What to bring, where to park
  • Your cancellation and weather policy

Who it is for

Jet ski and boat rentals, fishing charters, skydives, escape rooms, karting, axe throwing, climbing gyms, cooking and pottery classes, spas, ranges, and any local activity where the phone rings and people ask before they book. If you lose calls when you are busy running the thing you sell, Otto is for you.
