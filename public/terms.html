These terms cover two kinds of people: guests who book an experience through Outset, and the businesses whose activities are listed on it. Using the site means you accept them.

What Outset is

Outset is a place to find and book local activities: a kayak rental, a skydive, an escape room, range time, a cooking class. The activity itself is run by the business, not by us. The business sets its own prices, times, age limits, safety rules and refund policy, and is responsible for the experience it delivers.

Listings we built before the owner claimed them

Many pages on Outset were built from a business's own public website so guests can find it. Those pages carry only facts we could read on that site, and never invented prices, hours, availability or rules. A page is marked request-to-book until the owner claims it, and the owner can correct anything or ask us to remove it at any time by writing to hello@onoutset.com.

Booking and payment

Card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number. Where a listing is instant-book, your booking is confirmed as soon as payment succeeds. Where it is request-to-book, the business has to accept it first, and nothing is captured from your card until they do. Outset keeps 5% of a booking made through the site. There is no fee for a business to be listed, and no fee when there is no booking.

Cancellation and refunds

If you cancel, the business's own cancellation policy applies, and it is shown on the listing before you pay.

If the business cancels, you are refunded in full, automatically, to the card you paid with. You do not have to ask. A refund normally reaches your statement within 5 to 10 business days. If your card was authorised but not yet charged, the hold is released instead and nothing is taken.

Weather and safety

Activities on the water, in the air and outdoors get called off for weather, wind, lightning, unsafe water or a mechanical problem. That call belongs to the operator, and always to the operator: a captain, pilot or instructor may end or refuse an activity for safety at any point. When they call a booking off for weather or safety, it is the business cancelling, so the paragraph above applies in full: the guest is refunded automatically, with no fee to the guest and no commission to us. If only part of an experience ran, the business decides what to refund and we pass that decision through.

Your side of it

Give accurate names, numbers and guest counts, meet the age, weight, health and certification requirements printed on the listing, arrive on time, and follow the operator's safety instructions. An operator may refuse service on safety grounds, and where they do, their own policy decides the refund.

If you claim a listing, you confirm you are entitled to act for that business, and that the prices, hours, policies and photos you publish are accurate and yours to publish. You can unpublish or leave at any time. Bookings already taken still have to be honoured or cancelled and refunded.

We do our best to keep listings accurate, but a business can change a price or an opening time without telling us. Outset is not liable for the conduct of a business or a guest, for injury during an activity, or for a business closing. Nothing here takes away rights you have under consumer law where you live.
