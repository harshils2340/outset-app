Questions

How many bowling alleys are there in the US and Canada?

Outset lists 439 bowling alleys across the US and Canada. 419 of them have photos.

How much do bowling alleys cost in the US and Canada?

49 of the 439 operators publish prices on their own site. Starting prices run from $2 to $375. The full menu is on each listing.

How long do bowling alleys in the US and Canada take?

Listed durations include 2 hours, 1 hour and 3 hours, as stated on the operators' own sites.

Do the listings show opening hours?

142 of the 439 show hours copied from the operator's website. The rest do not publish them on Outset yet.

Where does this information come from?

From each operator's own website and public listings, one source per fact. Operators can claim their page on Outset and correct anything.
