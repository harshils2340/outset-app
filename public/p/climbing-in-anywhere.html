Questions

How many climbing gyms are there in the US and Canada?

Outset lists 187 climbing gyms across the US and Canada. 176 of them have photos.

How much do climbing gyms cost in the US and Canada?

43 of the 187 operators publish prices on their own site. Starting prices run from $3 to $395. The full menu is on each listing.

How long do climbing gyms in the US and Canada take?

Listed durations include 1 day, 1 hour and 1.5 hours, as stated on the operators' own sites.

Do the listings show opening hours?

56 of the 187 show hours copied from the operator's website. The rest do not publish them on Outset yet.

Where does this information come from?

From each operator's own website and public listings, one source per fact. Operators can claim their page on Outset and correct anything.
