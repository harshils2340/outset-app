What hot air balloon rides is actually like

Up before dawn, then drifting with the wind while the sun comes up and the ground goes gold.

  1. Meet in the dark, usually an hour before sunrise. The crew checks the wind and picks a launch field.
  2. You help hold the envelope while fans and burners inflate it. It's a show in itself.
  3. Climb into the basket. Liftoff is so gentle you may not notice you've left the ground.
  4. Float for about an hour. Balloons go where the wind goes, so every flight is a different route.
  5. Land in a field, sometimes with a bump. Many operators pour a toast after touchdown.

Bring: Layers, it's cold at dawn, Closed-toe shoes for wet grass, A hat, the burner is warm overhead, Camera with a strap. Good for: Anniversaries, proposals, and anyone who wants calm rather than adrenaline.
