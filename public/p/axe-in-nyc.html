What axe throwing is actually like

The satisfying thunk of a hatchet in a wooden target, and a bracket that gets competitive fast.

  1. Check in and your coach shows you the grip and the two-handed overhead throw.
  2. Practice for 10 minutes until you're sticking it. Most people land one within the first few throws.
  3. Play games like bullseye tournaments, 21, or cricket for the rest of the session.

Bring: Closed-toe shoes, Something comfortable to swing in, Photo ID if the venue serves drinks. Good for: Birthdays, team nights, and anyone who wants a competition that doesn't need athletic talent.
