What boat rentals are actually like

Your own boat for the day. Load the cooler, find a sandbar, and let everyone take a turn at the wheel.

  1. Check in at the marina and complete the boating checklist with staff.
  2. A walkthrough covers the controls, the chart, the no-wake zones, and where to anchor.
  3. Cruise out. Pontoons are wide and steady, so kids and grandparents are comfortable.
  4. Anchor, swim, snack, repeat. Return before your slot ends and fuel up if required.

Bring: Cooler with water and food, Sunscreen, hats, towels, A speaker, Boater card if your state requires it. Good for: Families, mixed-age groups, and birthdays that need room to spread out.
