Questions

How many ski areas are there in the US and Canada?

Outset lists 335 ski areas across the US and Canada. 276 of them have photos.

How much do ski areas cost in the US and Canada?

28 of the 335 operators publish prices on their own site. Starting prices run from $8 to $5,000. The full menu is on each listing.

How long do ski areas in the US and Canada take?

Listed durations include 1 day, 1 hour and 1.5 hours, as stated on the operators' own sites.

Do the listings show opening hours?

59 of the 335 show hours copied from the operator's website. The rest do not publish them on Outset yet.

Where does this information come from?

From each operator's own website and public listings, one source per fact. Operators can claim their page on Outset and correct anything.
