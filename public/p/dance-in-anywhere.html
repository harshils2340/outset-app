Questions

How many dance studios are there in the US and Canada?

Outset lists 1396 dance studios across the US and Canada. 821 of them have photos.

How much do dance studios cost in the US and Canada?

155 of the 1396 operators publish prices on their own site. Starting prices run from $5 to $550. The full menu is on each listing.

How long do dance studios in the US and Canada take?

Listed durations include 1 hour, 45 min and 30 min, as stated on the operators' own sites.

Do the listings show opening hours?

204 of the 1396 show hours copied from the operator's website. The rest do not publish them on Outset yet.

Where does this information come from?

From each operator's own website and public listings, one source per fact. Operators can claim their page on Outset and correct anything.
