Questions

How many shooting ranges are there in the US and Canada?

Outset lists 216 shooting ranges across the US and Canada. 197 of them have photos.

How much do shooting ranges cost in the US and Canada?

30 of the 216 operators publish prices on their own site. Starting prices run from $6 to $899. The full menu is on each listing.

How long do shooting ranges in the US and Canada take?

Listed durations include 2 hours, 1 hour and 7 days, as stated on the operators' own sites.

Do the listings show opening hours?

73 of the 216 show hours copied from the operator's website. The rest do not publish them on Outset yet.

Where does this information come from?

From each operator's own website and public listings, one source per fact. Operators can claim their page on Outset and correct anything.
