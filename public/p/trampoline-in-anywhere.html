How many trampoline parks are there in the US and Canada?

Outset lists 102 trampoline parks across the US and Canada. 87 of them have photos.

How much do trampoline parks cost in the US and Canada?

13 of the 102 operators publish prices on their own site. Starting prices run from $6.99 to $399. The full menu is on each listing.

How long do trampoline parks in the US and Canada take?

Listed durations include 2 hours, 60 min and 120 min, as stated on the operators' own sites.

30 of the 102 show hours copied from the operator's website. The rest do not publish them on Outset yet.
