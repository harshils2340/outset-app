Outset lists 210 water parks across the US and Canada. 194 of them have photos.

18 of the 210 operators publish prices on their own site. Starting prices run from $3 to $500. The full menu is on each listing.

Where does this information come from?

From each operator's own website and public listings, one source per fact. Operators can claim their page on Outset and correct anything.
