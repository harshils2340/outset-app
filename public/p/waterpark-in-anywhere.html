How many water parks are there in the US and Canada?

Outset lists 304 water parks across the US and Canada. 203 of them have photos.

19 of the 304 operators publish prices on their own site. Starting prices run from $3 to $500. The full menu is on each listing.

How long do water parks in the US and Canada take?

Listed durations include 3 hours, 2 days and 2 hours, as stated on the operators' own sites.

47 of the 304 show hours copied from the operator's website. The rest do not publish them on Outset yet.

Where does this information come from?

From each operator's own website and public listings, one source per fact. Operators can claim their page on Outset and correct anything.
