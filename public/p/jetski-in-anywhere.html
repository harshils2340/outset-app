What jet ski rentals is actually like

Throttle, spray and open water. The fastest way to feel like you own the bay.

  1. Check in at the dock, sign the waiver, and show your ID or boating card if required.
  2. A quick lesson covers the throttle, the kill cord, and where the riding area ends.
  3. Ride. Freestyle rentals give you a marked zone. Guided tours follow a leader to spots like sandbars or dolphin areas.
  4. Return to the dock a few minutes before your time is up.

Bring: Swimsuit and a towel, Waterproof sunscreen, Sunglasses with a strap, A dry bag for your phone. Good for: Couples riding two-up, friends racing each other, and beach days that need a jolt.

How much does it cost?

Published prices here start at $3.50. Most operators list a few options by duration or group size; the exact menu is on each listing.

Do I need to call to book?

No. Pick a listing, choose a time and the number of guests, and book. The operator confirms on Outset.

Where does this information come from?

From each operator's own website and public listings. Operators can claim their page and correct anything.
