How many martial arts gyms are there in the US and Canada?

How much do martial arts gyms cost in the US and Canada?

174 of the 993 operators publish prices on their own site. Starting prices run from $5 to $4,991. The full menu is on each listing.

How long do martial arts gyms in the US and Canada take?
