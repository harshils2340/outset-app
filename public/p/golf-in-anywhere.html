Questions

How many golf courses are there in the US and Canada?

Outset lists 5151 golf courses across the US and Canada. 2969 of them have photos.

How much do golf courses cost in the US and Canada?

256 of the 5151 operators publish prices on their own site. Starting prices run from $2 to $3,000. The full menu is on each listing.

How long do golf courses in the US and Canada take?

Listed durations include 30 min, 1 hour and 45 min, as stated on the operators' own sites.

Do the listings show opening hours?

649 of the 5151 show hours copied from the operator's website. The rest do not publish them on Outset yet.

Where does this information come from?

From each operator's own website and public listings, one source per fact. Operators can claim their page on Outset and correct anything.
