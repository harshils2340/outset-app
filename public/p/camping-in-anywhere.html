How many campgrounds are there in the US and Canada?

How much do campgrounds cost in the US and Canada?

728 of the 3037 operators publish prices on their own site. Starting prices run from $5 to $5,000. The full menu is on each listing.

How long do campgrounds in the US and Canada take?

Listed durations include 2 hours, 2 days and 1 hour, as stated on the operators' own sites.

Do the listings show opening hours?

Where does this information come from?

From each operator's own website and public listings, one source per fact. Operators can claim their page on Outset and correct anything.
