How many campgrounds are there in Portland?

Outset lists 29 campgrounds around Portland, including places in Portland, Scotts Mills, Jefferson and Dayton. 23 of them have photos.

8 of the 29 operators publish prices on their own site. Starting prices run from $7 to $675. The full menu is on each listing.

14 of the 29 show hours copied from the operator's website. The rest do not publish them on Outset yet.
