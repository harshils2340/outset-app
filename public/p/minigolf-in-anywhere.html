How many mini golf courses are there in the US and Canada?

Outset lists 239 mini golf courses across the US and Canada. 140 of them have photos.

How much do mini golf courses cost in the US and Canada?

18 of the 239 operators publish prices on their own site. Starting prices run from $4 to $450. The full menu is on each listing.

How long do mini golf courses in the US and Canada take?

Listed durations include 2 hours, 30 to 45 min and 3 hours, as stated on the operators' own sites.

Do the listings show opening hours?

43 of the 239 show hours copied from the operator's website. The rest do not publish them on Outset yet.

Where does this information come from?

From each operator's own website and public listings, one source per fact. Operators can claim their page on Outset and correct anything.
