Questions

How many zoos are there in the US and Canada?

Outset lists 229 zoos across the US and Canada. 221 of them have photos.

How much do zoos cost in the US and Canada?

30 of the 229 operators publish prices on their own site. Starting prices run from $2 to $800. The full menu is on each listing.

How long do zoos in the US and Canada take?

Listed durations include 2 hours, 90 min and 3 hours, as stated on the operators' own sites.

Do the listings show opening hours?

65 of the 229 show hours copied from the operator's website. The rest do not publish them on Outset yet.

Where does this information come from?

From each operator's own website and public listings, one source per fact. Operators can claim their page on Outset and correct anything.
