Questions

How many theme parks are there in the US and Canada?

Outset lists 302 theme parks across the US and Canada. 283 of them have photos.

How much do theme parks cost in the US and Canada?

55 of the 302 operators publish prices on their own site. Starting prices run from $2 to $672.86. The full menu is on each listing.

How long do theme parks in the US and Canada take?

Listed durations include 2 hours, 3 hours and 1 hour, as stated on the operators' own sites.

Do the listings show opening hours?

95 of the 302 show hours copied from the operator's website. The rest do not publish them on Outset yet.

Where does this information come from?

From each operator's own website and public listings, one source per fact. Operators can claim their page on Outset and correct anything.
