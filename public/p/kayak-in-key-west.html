What kayak rentals are actually like

Glassy water, mangrove tunnels or a quiet lake, and the feeling of moving under your own power.

  1. Check in at the launch and get fitted with a life jacket and paddle.
  2. A short lesson covers the grip, the forward stroke, and turning.
  3. Paddle out. Guided tours follow a route with wildlife stops. Rentals give you a map and a return time.
  4. Come back to the launch. Rinse off, and you're done.

Bring: Clothes that can get wet, Water shoes or sandals with a strap, Sunscreen and a hat, Dry bag for your phone. Good for: First-timers, families with kids 8 and up, and anyone who wants nature at a slow pace.
