Questions

How many wineries are there in the US and Canada?

Outset lists 1393 wineries across the US and Canada. 969 of them have photos.

How much do wineries cost in the US and Canada?

130 of the 1393 operators publish prices on their own site. Starting prices run from $2 to $5,000. The full menu is on each listing.

How long do wineries in the US and Canada take?

Listed durations include 2 hours, 90 min and 45 min, as stated on the operators' own sites.

Do the listings show opening hours?

394 of the 1393 show hours copied from the operator's website. The rest do not publish them on Outset yet.

Where does this information come from?

From each operator's own website and public listings, one source per fact. Operators can claim their page on Outset and correct anything.
