Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 September 2026. Outset, onoutset.com.

What we hold about a guest

When you book, we keep the name, email and phone number you type, what you booked, when, for how many people, and what it cost. We need those to confirm the booking and to tell the business you are coming. Card numbers are not among them: payment runs through Stripe, and we only ever see whether it succeeded.

What we hold about a business

Facts published on the business's own website: its name, address, phone number, public email, opening hours, services and prices, plus what its listing looks like on Outset. If the owner claims the listing, we also hold the name, work email and mobile they sign in with, and their payout details in Stripe.

Who it is shared with

A business you book with sees your name, contact details and booking. Our payments run through Stripe and our email through Resend; both see what they need to do that job and nothing else. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Email

We write to business owners about the page we built for their shop. Every one of those emails carries a one-click unsubscribe, and an address that unsubscribes is never written to again. Guests get email about their own bookings.

Cookies and measurement

The site stores your searches, saved listings and bookings in your own browser so the app works when you come back. That data stays on your device. We do not run advertising trackers.

Location

"Near me" asks your browser for your location, and only after you allow it. It is used to sort listings by distance in that moment, and is not stored on our servers.

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept while they are needed for the booking, for payouts and refunds, and for tax and accounting records. Unsubscribe records are kept indefinitely, because that is the only way to honour them.

Your choices

Write to hello@onoutset.com and we will tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. A business can have its listing removed from Outset the same way, without claiming it first. We answer within 30 days.
