Questions

About plans and pricing

Still have one? Talk to us

How much does it cost?

It depends on your plan, how many calls you get and how many locations you run. Book a quick demo and you'll get a price for your business on the call, with no obligation.

Is there a setup fee?

No. We set Outset up with you for free, and you call it yourself before it takes a real call.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. You pay monthly and can cancel anytime.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Move up or down whenever your business changes.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Your missed calls forward to Outset, or you can use a new number if you prefer.
